# Reading at N4

N4 continues to use the same hiragana and katakana learned at N5. There is no new alphabet to learn. The step up is reading longer sentences and recognizing more kanji and vocabulary in context.

## Build from N5

- Keep kana reading automatic; do not switch back to romaji when a passage becomes difficult.
- Learn the workbook’s [N4 kanji list](/JapaneseN4-kanji-reference) with words and example sentences.
- Use particles, verb endings, and context together to identify how a sentence is organized.
- Read short passages more than once: first for the general meaning, then for grammar and unknown vocabulary.

## Kanji reminder

A character can have multiple readings. The N4 reference table lists readings and meanings from the spreadsheet; it is a lookup aid, not a complete account of every reading or word. Learn the reading that appears in each vocabulary item.

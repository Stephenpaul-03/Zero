# Tally

Tally records personal income and expenses on the device, with no required account signup or bank connection. Workspaces separate currencies, categories, accounts, and budgets.

## What it does

- Transactions with categories, payment accounts, dates, and notes.
- Monthly and yearly insights and comparisons.
- Local backups with optional password encryption and validated imports.
- Appearance preferences, local reminders, and biometric locking on supported devices.
- Source modules also include goals, recurring activity, and summaries.

# GPA Calculator

A browser-based calculator for credit-weighted semester GPA and multi-semester CGPA. Subjects can be entered manually or loaded from an Excel preset, reducing repeated entry of course details.

## What it does

- Manual subject, credit, and grade entry.
- Excel presets with semester selection.
- A 10-point letter-grade scale and credit-weighted calculations.
- Excel and PDF export of calculated results.

# N4 Particles and Connectors

The workbook’s [N4 particle reference](/JapaneseN4-particles-reference) is separated from the N5 list. At this level, focus on how particles work in full clauses and how connectors show relationships between ideas.

## Common distinctions to practice

- **は and が:** topic versus subject/focus depends on the sentence and what is already known.
- **に and で:** に commonly marks a destination, time, or location of existence; で commonly marks where an action happens or the means used.
- **から and ので:** both can introduce reasons. ので often presents a reason more softly; the exact choice depends on context and register.
- **のに:** marks an unexpected contrast (“although”), not the same function as the noun-linking の plus に.
- **でも:** can mean “even” or “but/however” depending on how it is used.

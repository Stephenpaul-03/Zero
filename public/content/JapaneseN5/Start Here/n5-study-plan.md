# N5 Study Plan

## A steady sequence

- **Scripts:** Hiragana first, then katakana. Use short recognition and writing practice every day.
- **Sentence shape:** Learn topic, subject, object, place, and time marking through particles.
- **Forms:** Practice polite verbs, past and negative forms, て-form, and beginner adjective changes.
- **Vocabulary:** Study a small group, then make sentences using it.
- **Kanji:** Learn characters with readings in real words. The [N5 kanji reference](/JapaneseN5-kanji-reference) contains the workbook’s N5-labeled rows.

## Review loop

For a word or kanji, cover the meaning and recall it; then cover the Japanese and recall it from the meaning. Add a short sentence when possible. Revisit missed items after a delay rather than repeating only the ones already known.

## Moving to N4

When the basic N5 material is comfortable, use the [N4 course guide](/JapaneseN4-n4-course-guide). N4 expands reading and grammar; it does not replace the kana learned here.

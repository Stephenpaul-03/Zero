# N5 Particles

Particles follow a word or phrase and show how it connects to the rest of the sentence. The workbook’s [N5 particle reference](/JapaneseN5-particles-reference) includes its original meanings and examples where supplied.

| Particle | Common beginner use | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| は (wa) | Marks the topic | わたしは学生です。 — As for me, I am a student. |
| が (ga) | Marks a subject; can identify or emphasize it | だれが来ますか。 — Who is coming? |
| を (o) | Marks a direct object | 本を読みます。 — I read a book. |
| に (ni) | Marks a destination, time, or recipient in common patterns | 7時に起きます。 — I get up at 7. |
| で (de) | Marks where an action occurs or a means | 図書館で勉強します。 — I study at the library. |
| へ (e) | Marks direction | 日本へ行きます。 — I go to Japan. |
| の (no) | Connects nouns, often possession or description | わたしの本 — my book |
| と (to) | “And” with nouns; “with” in common patterns | 友だちと行きます。 — I go with a friend. |
| も (mo) | “Also / too” | わたしも学生です。 — I am a student too. |
| から / まで | From / until, to | 9時から5時まで — from 9 to 5 |

These are common uses, not one-to-one translations. A particle’s meaning depends on its sentence. Compare は and が in complete examples rather than memorizing a single English equivalent.

# Katakana

Katakana represents the same basic sound inventory as hiragana and is especially common in loanwords. The chart shows the basic gojūon rows in katakana. Read each kana as a mora, a rhythmic sound unit; vowel length and small kana matter.

## Voiced sounds

Dakuten (゛) changes consonants: カ→ガ, サ→ザ, タ→ダ, ハ→バ. Handakuten (゜) changes the H row to P sounds: ハ→パ.

## Combined kana

Small ゃ, ゅ, and ょ combine with an i-row kana: キャ (kya), シュ (shu), チョ (cho). The small っ indicates a brief closure before the next consonant, as in キッテ (kitte).

## Practice

Copy a row, cover it, and recall the sounds in both directions. Then read simple words without romaji. Do not treat the English spellings as exact pronunciation guides.

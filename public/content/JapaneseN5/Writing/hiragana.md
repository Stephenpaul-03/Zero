# Hiragana

Hiragana is the first script most learners study. The chart shows the basic gojūon rows. Read each kana as a mora, a rhythmic sound unit; vowel length and small kana matter.

## Voiced sounds

Dakuten (゛) changes consonants: か→が, さ→ざ, た→だ, は→ば. Handakuten (゜) changes the H row to P sounds: は→ぱ.

## Combined kana

Small ゃ, ゅ, and ょ combine with an i-row kana: きゃ (kya), しゅ (shu), ちょ (cho). The small っ indicates a brief closure before the next consonant, as in きって (kitte).

## Practice

Copy a row, cover it, and recall the sounds in both directions. Then read simple words without romaji. Do not treat the English spellings as exact pronunciation guides.

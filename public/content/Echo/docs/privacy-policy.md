# Echo Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Stephen Paul** (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use **Echo** (the “App”). Echo is a fullscreen Android now-playing companion for Poweramp and compatible media players.

## 1. Information Echo accesses

When you enable a connection, Echo may access the following information from an active media player on your Android device:

- track title, artist, album, duration, playback position, playback state, queue context, and media/session identifiers;
- album artwork exposed by the media player; and
- the active media session needed to show controls and change playback or volume.

If you choose the notification/media-session connection, Android requires Notification Access. Echo uses that access to observe media-session information and provide the now-playing interface. Echo does not use it to read, store, or send unrelated notification content.

If you use the Poweramp connection, Echo uses Poweramp’s supported integration and Android media APIs to read the information needed for the App’s now-playing features.

## 2. What Echo does not do

Based on the current release implementation, Echo does not:

- require an account, name, email address, contacts, location, microphone, camera, or payment information;
- collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or cross-app tracking data;
- upload your music metadata, artwork, queue, or playback activity to an Echo server;
- sell or share personal information with advertisers or data brokers; or
- download or store music files.

## 3. On-device processing and local settings

The App processes now-playing information on your device to render the interface and send controls to the selected media session. Display preferences and other App settings may be stored locally on your device. We do not receive those local settings.

The Android operating system, your media player, and your device manufacturer may separately collect or process information under their own policies.

## 4. Network access and third parties

The current Echo implementation does not include an Echo backend, account system, analytics service, advertising SDK, crash-reporting service, or payment processor. This statement must be updated before release if any dependency, SDK, update mechanism, website, or support flow transmits information.

Google Play and the infrastructure used to host this policy may process ordinary technical request data, such as IP address, timestamp, user-agent, and requested URL, under their own terms and policies.

## 5. Retention and deletion

Echo does not maintain an Echo account or a server-side profile. On-device media/session data is used while needed for the feature and is released when the connection or App lifecycle ends, subject to Android and media-player behavior. You can revoke Notification Access in Android Settings and uninstall Echo to remove the App and its local data.

If a later release introduces accounts, server-side storage, analytics, crash reports, or other collection, this section must be replaced with the applicable retention and deletion rules before that release.

## 6. Security

We limit Echo’s processing to the features described above and do not intentionally transmit media data to us. No software can guarantee absolute security. Keep Android, Echo, and your media player updated and only grant Notification Access if you want the fallback connection.
